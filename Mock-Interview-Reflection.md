# Reflection on Mock Interview Experience

## STAR Method

### Situation

As part of my work readiness training, I participated in a mock interview designed to simulate a real recruitment process for an entry-level software development position. The interview required me to introduce myself professionally, discuss my technical skills, explain my academic projects, and answer behavioural questions while maintaining confidence and professionalism.

### Task

My goal was to demonstrate my technical knowledge, communicate my experiences clearly, and present myself as a capable and professional candidate. I also wanted to identify areas where I could improve before attending real interviews for internships and graduate software development positions.

### Action

I prepared by reviewing my CV, reflecting on my academic projects, and practising responses to common interview questions. During the interview, I focused on speaking clearly, maintaining a professional attitude, and providing examples from my education, previous work experience, and technical projects to support my answers. I also made an effort to explain technical concepts in a way that would be understandable to interviewers with different levels of technical knowledge.

### Result

The mock interview significantly improved my confidence in discussing my skills and experiences in a professional setting. It highlighted the importance of preparation, effective communication, and supporting answers with practical examples. I also recognised areas where I can continue improving, particularly by providing more detailed technical explanations and reducing nervousness when answering unexpected questions. Overall, the experience better prepared me for future interviews and strengthened my readiness to begin my career in the software development industry.
